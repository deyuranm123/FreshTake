# SOC Type 1 and Type 2 Reports

## What is a SOC Report?

**SOC** stands for **System and Organization Controls** (formerly known as Service Organization Controls). SOC reports are independent, third-party audit reports that assess the internal controls of a service organization. They are issued by a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) or an accredited auditing firm and are governed by standards set by the **American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA)**.

Organizations use SOC reports to demonstrate to their customers and business partners that they have robust controls in place to protect data security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

---

## SOC Type 1 Report

### Definition
A **SOC Type 1 report** evaluates the **design and implementation** of a service organization's controls **at a specific point in time**.

### Key Characteristics
- **Point-in-time assessment**: The audit captures a snapshot of controls on a single date (e.g., December 31).
- **Design suitability**: Auditors assess whether the controls are *suitably designed* to meet the stated control objectives or trust service criteria.
- **No operational testing**: It does **not** test whether controls were operating effectively over a period of time.

### Purpose
- Provides initial assurance to customers that controls exist and are properly designed.
- Useful for new service organizations or those just beginning their compliance journey.
- Helps identify gaps in control design before pursuing a more rigorous Type 2 audit.

### Who Uses It?
- Organizations that want to quickly demonstrate baseline security posture to clients or prospects.
- Companies preparing for a SOC Type 2 audit in the future.

---

## SOC Type 2 Report

### Definition
A **SOC Type 2 report** evaluates both the **design** and the **operational effectiveness** of controls **over a period of time**, typically a minimum of six months (often 12 months).

### Key Characteristics
- **Period-of-time assessment**: The audit covers a defined timeframe (e.g., January 1 – December 31).
- **Operational effectiveness**: Auditors test whether the controls were consistently and effectively operating throughout the review period.
- **More rigorous**: Requires evidence of consistent control execution, not just documentation that controls exist.

### Purpose
- Provides stronger assurance to customers and partners that controls are not only designed correctly but are also functioning as intended.
- Widely required by enterprise customers before they will do business with a vendor.
- Often required for compliance with regulations such as HIPAA, SOX, and GDPR.

### Who Uses It?
- SaaS companies, cloud service providers, data centers, and other technology service organizations.
- Organizations subject to regulatory requirements or enterprise customer due diligence requirements.

---

## Key Differences: SOC Type 1 vs. SOC Type 2

| Feature | SOC Type 1 | SOC Type 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Assessment period** | Point in time (single date) | Period of time (6–12+ months) |
| **What is evaluated** | Design of controls | Design + operational effectiveness of controls |
| **Testing depth** | No operational testing | Extensive testing of control operation |
| **Level of assurance** | Lower | Higher |
| **Time to complete** | Shorter (weeks to a few months) | Longer (at least 6 months of evidence) |
| **Common use case** | Initial compliance, new companies | Enterprise vendor due diligence, regulatory compliance |

---

## Types of SOC Reports

There are multiple categories of SOC reports beyond just Type 1 and Type 2:

- **SOC 1**: Focused on controls relevant to **financial reporting** (used by service organizations that impact their clients' financial statements).
- **SOC 2**: Focused on the **Trust Service Criteria**: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy.
- **SOC 3**: A public-facing, simplified version of the SOC 2 report intended for general audiences.

Both SOC 1 and SOC 2 can be issued as either a **Type 1** or **Type 2** report.
